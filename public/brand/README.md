# AGILA brand assets

Code-native vector adaptation of the user-supplied AGILA brand board. Monochrome eagle/A mark, geometric wordmark, and app icons. No downloaded logo or external image service is used.

AGILA: Advanced Geospatial Intelligence for Life Safety and Alerts. Black and white identity; charcoal matte glass surfaces. Operational maps retain their natural colors, and danger/warning indicators retain semantic red/amber colors.

Regenerate icons with `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`. App identifiers and storage namespaces remain stable for upgrade compatibility.
